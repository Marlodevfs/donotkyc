Reaching a blocked exchange with a VPN

  1. Pick a provider from the list. Some links are affiliate links.
  2. Download and install the VPN software on your device.
  3. Connect to a server where your desired exchange is not blocked.
  4. Open the exchange and trade. A VPN hides your IP address; it does not remove the exchange's own KYC rules.
