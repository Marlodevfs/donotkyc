How the directory works

Each listing shows the identity the platform asks for, its limits, the regions it blocks, its fees and whether it takes card or bank deposits. We re-tag or remove a listing when the platform makes ID mandatory.

  • Exchanges

    High liquidity, order books and mobile apps. Their no-KYC tiers cap withdrawals for unverified accounts.

  • Decentralised exchanges

    Smart contracts, no sign-up. You trade from your own wallet and keep your keys.

  • Instant swaps

    Quick coin-to-coin conversions without an account, the spread built into the quote.

  • P2P markets

    You pay another person with a local payment method. Safety depends on escrow and reputation.

  • ATMs and bridges

    ATMs sell crypto for cash, without ID under a threshold. Bridges move assets between blockchains.

Choosing a no-KYC platform

Start from how much privacy you need and how comfortable you are with wallets. A decentralised exchange gives the most control and anonymity; an exchange with a no-KYC tier is easier and has deeper liquidity.

Compare the total cost

Look at trading fees, spreads and network fees together rather than the headline fee. Check that the coins and networks you need are supported, and that the platform has a clear history and answers when something goes wrong.

Test small, then move

Make a small test deposit, trade and withdrawal before committing larger amounts. Spread counterparty risk across platforms and keep long-term holdings in self-custody.

Stay safe

Use a hardware or well-audited software wallet, check URLs and contract addresses, grant wallet permissions only when needed and avoid large custodial balances. No-KYC does not remove your legal or tax obligations, and it reduces your recourse if something goes wrong.

Common questions

What is KYC?

Know Your Customer is the identity check regulated services run before letting you trade: ID card or passport, proof of address, sometimes a selfie. It exists for anti-money-laundering rules, and it concentrates sensitive data that can leak in a breach.

Is trading without KYC legal?

It depends on where you live. Using a platform that does not ask for ID is legal in most countries, but tax and reporting rules still apply to you. Decentralised protocols face less direct oversight; you stay responsible for following your local law.

What do None, Light and Tiered mean?

None: no ID and no account details. Light: no ID under a stated limit, or only an email or phone number. Tiered: ID is required above a threshold, written on each listing.

Which type of platform is best for beginners?

Start with an exchange that has a no-KYC tier: it works like the apps you know. A decentralised exchange gives the most privacy and control, but you need to be comfortable with a wallet and network fees.

How do fees compare?

Exchanges have tighter spreads and fast internal transfers. Decentralised exchanges show lower protocol fees but add network costs. Instant swaps build their margin into the quote, and ATMs add a cash-handling markup.

How does DoNotKYC choose platforms?

We check each listing against the platform’s documentation and how the app behaves, then review it again over time. If a service removes no-KYC access or makes ID mandatory for its core features, we re-tag or remove it.
