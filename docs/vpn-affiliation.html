VPNs for Buying Crypto on Blocked Sites

Some countries block popular crypto exchanges. A VPN (virtual private network) hides your IP address from the exchange and lets you reach it from another country.

Below are the VPN providers we would look at first.

Top Recommended VPN Providers

  • NordVPN - Large network of servers, paid plans. Get NordVPN
  • Proton VPN - Swiss provider with a no-logs policy and a free tier. Get ProtonVPN
  • Obscura VPN - Built so the provider cannot link your traffic to your payment, rather than relying on a no-logs promise. Get Obscura VPN
  • Mullvad - No email needed to sign up, accepts cash and Monero. Get Mullvad
  • Tunnelbear - Simple apps and a small free plan. Get Tunnelbear
  • Meile DVPN - Decentralised VPN running on the Sentinel network. Get Meile DVPN
  • LNVPN - Pay per hour with Lightning, no account. Get LNVPN
  • Xeovo - No-logs VPN that accepts crypto payments. Get Xeovo

How to Use a VPN with Crypto Exchanges?

  1. Pick a provider from the list. Some links are affiliate links.
  2. Download and install the VPN software on your device.
  3. Connect to a server where your desired exchange is not blocked.
  4. Open the exchange and trade. A VPN hides your IP address; it does not remove the exchange's own KYC rules.

Important Legal Notice

VPN use and crypto trading regulations vary by country. Ensure compliance with your local laws before using a VPN to access crypto exchanges.
