Decentralised exchanges

A decentralised exchange runs on smart contracts. You connect your own wallet and trade without an account, so there is no identity to give and nobody holds your coins.

You pay network fees on top of the trading fee, and you need to be comfortable with a wallet. Some front-ends block certain countries even though the contracts stay open.
