Privacy policy

DoNotKYC does not sell data and keeps no personal data about you.

What the site does not use

  • No advertising trackers, no cookies.
  • Fonts, images and code come from www.donotkyc.com. The only outside script is the visit counter below.
  • Nothing you type on this site is sent to us. Submissions and corrections go through GitHub, under GitHub's own privacy statement.

What stays on your device

If you switch between light and dark theme, your browser remembers the choice in local storage. Site search also runs in your browser: your queries go nowhere.

Visit counter

We count visits with GoatCounter, an open-source counter that uses no cookies. It records which page was opened, the referring site, browser, country and screen size, never your name or anything that follows you across sites. We use it to know how many people read the site.

Hosting

The site runs on GitHub Pages. GitHub logs the IP address of visitors for security purposes. We have no access to those logs. GitHub's explanation.

Links to other sites

When you follow a platform link, you leave DoNotKYC and that site's policy applies. An affiliate link tells the platform you came from us; it tells us nothing about you. See how we make money.

Changes

Changes to this policy appear in the changelog. Last updated 19 October 2026.
