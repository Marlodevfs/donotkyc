Bitcoin ATMs with low or no ID

Bitcoin ATMs sell crypto for cash. Many allow small purchases with a phone number or nothing at all, then ask for ID above a threshold set by the operator and local rules.

ATM fees run several percent above the market price. They are most useful for small amounts when cash is what you have.
