VPN providers

A VPN hides your IP address from the platform you use and your traffic from your internet provider. You now trust the VPN company instead.

Check how you pay and what the company says it logs.

Order: what the tool asks of you (nothing, an account, ID), then open code before closed code, then A to Z. Affiliate links and sponsors never change it.

    • Says it has had eight audits since 2019.
    • Says of itself: "We don't promise anonymity."
    • IVPN Limited is registered in Gibraltar.

Reaching a blocked exchange with a VPN

  1. Pick a provider from the list. Some links are affiliate links.
  2. Download and install the VPN software on your device.
  3. Connect to a server where your desired exchange is not blocked.
  4. Open the exchange and trade. A VPN hides your IP address; it does not remove the exchange's own KYC rules.

Legal notice

Rules on VPN use and crypto trading differ by country. Check your local law before you use a VPN to reach an exchange.
