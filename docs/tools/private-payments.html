Private payments

Ways to pay online without giving a merchant your card or phone number.

Some of them ask for ID. The entry says so.

Order: what the tool asks of you (nothing, an account, ID), then open code before closed code, then A to Z. Affiliate links and sponsors never change it.

  1. MySudo

    Asks of you: nothing Code: not stated
    • App that gives each identity its own phone number, email address and virtual cards.
    • Says it never requires your personal email or phone number to get started.
    • Phone numbers in the United States, United Kingdom and Canada (limited). UK numbers require legal identity information.

    Visit MySudo Checked 2026-10-19

  2. Privacy.com

    Asks of you: ID Code: not stated
    • Virtual cards that hide your real payment details from merchants.
    • United States residents only. Requires a KYC check and a US bank account.

    Visit Privacy.com Checked 2026-10-19

  3. Coincards

    Asks of you: not stated Code: not stated
    • Buy gift cards, top-ups and prepaid vouchers with Bitcoin. No bank required.
    • Over 20 countries.
    • Its page does not mention accounts or KYC.

    Visit Coincards Checked 2026-10-19
