What we check before listing

  • The platform's own terms, FAQ or help pages state its KYC policy and limits. A link to that source is required.
  • The site works, and its policy matches what users report.
  • No credible reports of exit scams, frozen funds without reason, or fake volume.
  • The identity level (None, Light or Tiered) can be stated clearly.

A listing is wrong or out of date

If a listed platform now asks for ID, changed its limits or fees, blocks new countries or closed, report the change. Every report is checked before the listing is updated.

Privacy

Both forms are public GitHub issues and need a GitHub account. Do not include personal data, and remove names or account numbers from screenshots. A form that needs no account will come when the site moves to its own server.
