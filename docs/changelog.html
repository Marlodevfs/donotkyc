Changelog

Every change to the directory, dated. Subscribe with RSS.

Tools: operating systems, e-mail and aliases

19 October 2026. Four more tool pages: desktop systems (Tails, Whonix, Qubes OS and more), phone systems (GrapheneOS, CalyxOS, /e/OS, LineageOS), private e-mail providers and e-mail alias services. Same entry format and same sort rule as the other tool pages.

Tools section: VPN, Tor, DNS, wallets and more

19 October 2026. Ten new pages list the tools people use next to a no-KYC platform: VPNs, Tor and other networks, encrypted DNS, leak tests, routers, Bitcoin, hardware and Monero wallets, privacy coins and private payments. Each entry quotes the maker's own site, says what the tool asks of you and whether its code is open, and follows one sort rule. The old VPN guide now lives on the VPN page.

KYC claims corrected for 4 platforms

19 October 2026. We compared our listings with the platforms' own policies. Changelly and StealthEX publish identity-check policies, so they no longer read "email only" or "no ID up to $700". LocalCoinSwap can ask for ID after a dispute or an alert. Bybit lets unverified accounts withdraw up to 20,000 USDT a day but asks for ID to trade. Seven listings now link the policy page they rely on.

Versioned API and security contact

19 October 2026. The directory is now also served at /api/v1/platforms.json, one flat list with every field. A security.txt file tells researchers where to report a problem, and robots.txt names the AI crawlers that are welcome.

Cookie-free visit counter

19 October 2026. DoNotKYC now counts visits with GoatCounter, an open-source counter with no cookies and no personal data. The privacy policy explains what it records.

Synapse back to active

18 October 2026. synapseprotocol.com now redirects to Hypercall, the team's new product, but the Synapse bridge still works at bridge.synapseprotocol.com. The listing points there and is no longer under review.

How we make money, and a privacy policy

18 October 2026. Two new pages explain who pays DoNotKYC (affiliate links and labelled sponsored tiles, with our advertising rules) and confirm the site collects no data about visitors.

Boltz under review: swaps suspended

18 October 2026. Boltz suspended its swaps on 3 August 2026 and its website no longer resolves. The listing stays visible with a warning and is out of our recommendations until the service is back.

Synapse under review

18 October 2026. synapseprotocol.com now redirects to hypercall.xyz/synapse. We are confirming the service and its address before updating the listing.

1inch moved to 1inch.com

18 October 2026. The listing now points to the new domain.

Weekly automatic check of every listing

18 October 2026. Every Monday a script checks that each platform's site still answers and has not moved to another domain, and lists platforms whose last real-money test is older than 90 days.

Side-by-side comparisons

18 October 2026. 18 comparisons of platforms that do the same job, such as MEXC vs Bybit or Bisq vs Hodl Hodl.

Site available in 9 languages

18 October 2026. English, French, Spanish, German, Portuguese, Italian, Russian, Turkish and Chinese.

New directory, one page per platform

18 October 2026. Platforms are now sorted by how much identity they ask for (None, Light, Tiered), with a page each, topic pages and alternatives to exchanges that require ID. Data errors fixed for KCEX, DeGate and Boltz. Google Analytics and third-party logos removed: the site makes no request to any other service.
