Advertise on DoNotKYC

DoNotKYC is read by people who want to trade crypto without handing over their identity. A sponsored tile puts your service next to the platforms they compare, with a Sponsored label.

Contact us on Telegram

Placements

  • Homepage tile: one sponsor at a time, in the "Where we would start" section.
  • Topic page tile: one sponsor per page, above the list. Pages include Exchanges, Decentralised exchanges, Instant swaps, P2P markets, Bitcoin ATMs, Bridges, Bitcoin, Monero, Cash, Bank transfer, Card.

Placements run by calendar month. Prepaying 3 or 12 months reduces the monthly rate. Ask for current rates and audience figures.

The rules

  • Every tile says "Sponsored" and links with rel="sponsored".
  • Paying does not change a listing, its identity level or its position in the directory.
  • We review every advertiser before accepting. We refuse services with credible scam or frozen-funds reports, and anything illegal where it is offered.
  • We can remove a tile if new evidence of harm to users appears. Conditions are agreed before payment.

How it works

  1. Send us your service, the placement you want and the dates on Telegram.
  2. We review the service and reply within a few days.
  3. You receive a crypto invoice (Bitcoin, Lightning or Monero).
  4. Once paid, the tile goes live on the start date and comes down on the end date automatically. We remind you before it ends.
