No-KYC crypto exchanges

Centralised exchanges give you order books, deep liquidity, futures and mobile apps. The ones below let you trade without an ID document, usually up to a daily withdrawal limit.

They hold your funds while they are on the platform. Withdraw to your own wallet when you are done, and check the limit before depositing more than it allows.

Questions

Can an exchange ask for ID later?

Yes. Most no-KYC tiers can trigger a check on large withdrawals or unusual activity, and policies change. Start small and keep only what you are trading on the exchange.
